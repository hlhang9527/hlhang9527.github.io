Welcome! I am Huiliang Zhang, a research scientist with a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from McGill University, advised by Prof. Benoit Boulet. I am a Senior Research Scientist at AI4Alpha (Data Labs) and a Research Consultant in applied ML/RL at Protago Labs.

I build reliable AI systems for decision-making in complex workflows. My work spans agent and model evaluation, RL environments and post-training data, agentic AI products, applied machine learning and reinforcement learning, and spatial-temporal machine learning.

My research and product work asks one central question: how can AI systems help people and organizations make better decisions under uncertainty, while remaining measurable, auditable, and deployable?

## Focus Areas

- **[Agent and model evaluation](/focus-areas/#agent-and-model-evaluation):** expert benchmarks that find where frontier LLMs and agents still fail.
- **[RL environments and post-training data](/focus-areas/#rl-environments-and-post-training-data):** turning real repositories and databases into checkable training tasks for agents.
- **[Agentic AI products](/focus-areas/#agentic-ai-products):** the agent harness behind UCoWorker, an AI coworker for long-running knowledge work.
- **[Applied ML/RL for financial decisions](/focus-areas/#applied-mlrl-for-financial-decisions):** collection outreach and fraud detection for consumer lending.
- **[Spatial-temporal representation learning](/focus-areas/#spatial-temporal-representation-learning):** forecasting with the whole history instead of a short recent window.
- **[Graph learning and safe RL for energy and control](/focus-areas/#graph-learning-and-safe-rl-for-energy-and-control):** coordinating buildings for lower energy cost under safety limits.
